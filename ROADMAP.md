# Roadmap

1. Complete Codex and Claude Code installation, positive/negative trigger, update and uninstall evidence before stable status.
2. Evaluate host schema adapters against upstream fixtures; avoid replacing official validators.
3. Collect pilot feedback and compare against existing validators before broadening scope.
4. Qualify the maintenance evidence and configuration contract hypotheses with independent demand.
5. Work toward three new projects or Skills published per day (12h,18h,21h São Paulo; stop new publication at three), as requested by the user, after exact-file review, clean tests, confirmed destination and remote verification. Group related Skills; do not count duplicates or bypass quality gates.

No dates or adoption metrics are promised. Usage is not collected. Public issues, external contributions and consented user feedback will be measured once repositories exist.

Experimental CI evidence scope: collect feedback on expected app/context policies before adding legacy commit-status APIs, automatic policy discovery or remediation. No claims of branch-protection equivalence.

Source archive review: keep ZIP limits and operator expectations explicit; evaluate actual prerequisite policies before TAR support or automation. No LFS hydration or automatic archive-setting changes.

MCP structured result review: obtain consented real integration feedback and compare with Inspector/SDK diagnostics before expanding beyond the pinned legacy profile. Format assertions and modern protocol support require separate specifications and tests.
