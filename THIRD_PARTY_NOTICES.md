The original Ancover source in this repository is MIT licensed.

Runtime dependency: **yaml 2.9.1**, declared license **ISC**, verified in its installed package metadata. Source and licensing: [eemeli/yaml](https://github.com/eemeli/yaml). Its source is not vendored in this release; installation retrieves it with lockfile integrity verification. Preserve upstream notices if redistributing installed dependencies.

Quality reference: [morluto/rea](https://github.com/morluto/rea), MIT licensed at inspection. Read-only study informed the quality matrix; no source code, prose or assets were copied into the Ancover package. Dependency advisory checks and licensing observations are point-in-time evidence, not a legal opinion or security certification.
